## 1:10–2:10 · Preview the hypothetical order

**Screen.** Enable Robert’s saved NVIDIA preview. Compare the position’s weight and risk contribution. Open the Canadian-dollar replay. Focus the chart on the 2022 low and point to the balance floor and duration below it. Use evidence values from the displayed result.

**Narration.** Robert is considering a 40 percent allocation to NVIDIA. In this historical model it accounts for about 90 percent of the portfolio’s risk. EPICENTRE starts both mixes at the same fictional $600,000 and replays them over the same dates. The proposed mix was worth about $408,000 at its low. His assumed limit of $90,000 in losses puts his balance floor at $510,000. The replay shows how long the proposed mix would have spent below that line. Robert can use that evidence before making his own decision.

## 2:10–3:05 · Let the investor ask why

**Screen.** Show an already calculated evidence item with its method and provenance. In the API demo, select a loss-oriented focus, then ask for the explanation. Show the selected evidence item and the result’s mode. If the backend returns a rules fallback, retain the visible mode label.

**Narration.** The application calculates the figures first. With a retirement goal, the explanation can point Robert to the loss evidence. Amazon Bedrock’s role is to select from the evidence items already available. The server verifies the selection and shows the original values and their sources. The model does not receive the underlying holdings or price rows. A rules-based selection is available if the model cannot respond.
